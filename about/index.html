About

My path into design began with fine arts in Novi Sad and later digital art in Verona. I’ve always been curious about how visual choices shape the way we understand and experience things.
Over time, that curiosity moved beyond the image itself and toward interactions, systems, and the people using them.

Product design became a natural place for those interests to meet. I enjoy research, visual thinking, and putting ideas together until a clear direction starts to take shape.
My work centers on emerging tech, creative tools, and design systems.

AI in my workflow

I use agentic workflows to support research, prototyping, and design system work.
AI helps me audit interfaces, identify inconsistencies, document components, structure tokens and variants, and move ideas into something testable earlier.
I see it as a way to handle more of the repetitive and analytical work while keeping the important decisions around hierarchy, behavior, accessibility, and consistency deliberate.
