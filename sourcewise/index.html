LEARNINGS

What I learned

01

I had defined the use case too narrowly

I designed around comparing multiple quotes, but users also saw value in organizing a single supplier offer.

02

Designing for trust

Users did not reject AI help. They needed to see where each value came from, and that reshaped most screens built after research.

03

I was showing too much by default

Every instinct to show everything worked against the goal of keeping things simple for a non specialist. Testing usually told me which way to move.

RESEARCH LIMITATIONS

I mocked AI outputs to test how users would verify them, and treated three exploratory interviews as directional input rather than representative evidence.
